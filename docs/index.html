Velo Fitness

Privacy Policy  ·  Effective date: May 2025

The short version: Velo Fitness stores all your data locally on your device. Nothing is collected, transmitted, or shared with any third party beyond the Strava and Garmin servers you explicitly sign in to.

What data the app accesses

Velo Fitness connects to the following external services only when you initiate a sync:

  • Strava — cycling activity metadata (name, date, distance, time, elevation, heart rate, power, cadence, speed). Fetched via the official Strava API using OAuth 2.0.
  • Garmin Connect (beta) — the same activity metadata fields. Fetched via Garmin's Connect API using your account credentials. This integration is unofficial and not affiliated with Garmin Ltd.

No other data sources are accessed. The app does not access your location, camera, contacts, photos, microphone, or any other device capability.

How data is stored

  • All ride data is stored locally on your device in the app's private Documents folder as a rides.json file.
  • Strava OAuth tokens (access token, refresh token, expiry) are stored in iOS UserDefaults, which is private to this app.
  • Garmin OAuth tokens are stored in iOS UserDefaults. Your Garmin password is used only during sign-in and is never stored by this app.
  • No data is stored in iCloud, any external server, or any database operated by this app's developer.

Data sharing and third parties

Velo Fitness does not:

  • Sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party
  • Use analytics SDKs or crash-reporting services
  • Display advertising
  • Track you across apps or websites

The only outbound network requests the app makes are to www.strava.com and connectapi.garmin.com at your explicit request during a sync.

Strava data

By connecting your Strava account you agree to Strava's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. You can revoke Velo Fitness's access to your Strava account at any time at strava.com/settings/apps.

Garmin data (beta)

The Garmin integration uses an unofficial API and is provided as a beta feature. It is not endorsed by or affiliated with Garmin Ltd. By using this feature you accept that it may stop working at any time and that your use is subject to Garmin's Terms of Service.

Data deletion

To delete all data collected by this app:

  • Delete the app from your device — this removes rides.json and all stored tokens.
  • To revoke Strava access separately, visit strava.com/settings/apps.

Children's privacy

Velo Fitness is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the effective date above will be updated. Continued use of the app after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
